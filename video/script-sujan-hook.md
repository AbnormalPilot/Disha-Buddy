# Sujan — the hook

**23 words. About 10 seconds. Voice only, no camera.**

This is the opening of the film, 0:00 to 0:10. It is the single most important
line in the whole thing: it is what the judges hear before they hear anything
about the product.

A phone voice memo from bed is completely fine.

---

## What to say

> "Bhaiya, seventy-eight percent aaye. Ab kya karun?"
>
> *(stop. one full beat of silence.)*
>
> Fifteen lakh Indian students ask that question every year.
> Almost none of them get an answer.

---

## How to say it

- The first line is **a student texting, not a narrator reading a quote.** A
  seventeen-year-old who just got their result and is worried. Slightly rushed,
  slightly flat.
- **Then actually stop.** A full second of nothing. That silence is the hook; the
  line does not work without it.
- The last two sentences are said plainly, no drama. "Almost none of them get an
  answer" is the sentence that has to land. Say it and stop.

**A rough or tired voice is not a problem here.** If anything it suits the line —
it is a stressed student's question, not an announcer's. Do not try to sound
polished.

---

## Recording

- Voice Memos, phone 15 to 20 cm from your mouth, slightly off to the side.
- Quiet room, fan and AC off.
- Start recording, **wait 2 seconds**, speak, then leave 2 seconds at the end.
- Say it **twice in one take** with a three-second gap. The better one gets used.
- Send the original `.m4a`. Not through WhatsApp, it crushes the audio.

Total effort: about a minute. If you are too unwell, say so and Himanshu will
cover it, but this is 23 words and your voice suits it better than his.
