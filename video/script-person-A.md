## What to say

### 1. The hook (about 10 seconds)

> "Bhaiya, seventy-eight percent aaye. Ab kya karun?"
>
> *(stop. one full beat of silence.)*
>
> Fifteen lakh Indian students ask that question every year.
> Almost none of them get an answer.

Say the Hinglish line **the way a nervous 17-year-old would text it** — not as a
narrator reading a quote. Then genuinely stop. That silence is the hook of the
whole film, and it only works if you leave it.

### 2. Why nobody answers (about 19 seconds)

> Ninety-three percent of Indian schools have no career counsellor.
> Where there is one, they carry three thousand students.
> India needs about fourteen lakh counsellors. It has under ten thousand.
> So the students who need guidance most, first-generation, small-town, no money
> for private counselling, get none of it.

Four flat facts, no drama, no adjectives. Slow down on **"under ten thousand"**.
Let the last sentence land heavier than the rest.

### 3. What we built (about 20 seconds)

> So we built Disha.
> It lives on Telegram, talks like students talk, and remembers what matters:
> marks, budget, stream, every deadline.
> It names colleges that fit, with the source and date next to every number.
> And it doesn't wait to be asked. Before a round closes, Disha texts you first.

**"Disha texts you first"** is the sentence the entire film is built on. Small
pause before it, say it plainly, and stop. Don't lift your voice at the end.

## How to record

- **Voice Memos on your phone is fine.** No camera, no setup.
- Quiet room. Fan off, AC off, notifications off. A small soft room with curtains
  or a bed in it beats a big empty one — less echo.
- Hold the phone **15 to 20 cm from your mouth**, slightly off to the side so your
  "p" sounds don't thump.
- **Start recording, wait 2 seconds, then speak.** Leave 2 seconds at the end too.
- Read the whole thing **twice in one take** with a three-second gap between. The
  better one gets used.
- Record a **3-second clip of just your silent room** at the end, so the audio can
  be noise-matched to the other two parts.

**Send the original file** (`.m4a` from Voice Memos is perfect). Do not send it
through WhatsApp — it crushes the audio, and audio is the entire job here.
