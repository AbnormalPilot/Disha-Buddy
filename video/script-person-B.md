# Person B — your recording

One clip. **Video and voice together**, on your phone. Your voice becomes the
narration and your face becomes the circle in the bottom-right corner of the film.

Your section is **26 seconds**, sitting at 0:49 to 1:15. It's the demo: the phone
is on screen the whole time showing a real conversation, so you're narrating
something that is happening, not describing a feature.

---

## What to say

> Riya types one line. Seventy-eight percent, budget one lakh, UP.
>
> Seconds later she has colleges she can actually get into, with fees, cutoffs,
> and a link to where each number came from.
>

>
> Two days later, Disha messages her first:
>

>

57 words. Read it as written if you can — the on-screen subtitles are set to these

## Delivery

- **Say the two Hinglish bits the way people actually say them.** "Seventy-eight
  percent, budget one lakh, UP" is how a student texts. "Riya, AKTU round two kal
  band ho raha hai" is Disha's message. Neither should sound like a narrator
  reading a quote.
- **The gap before "Two days later" is the point of the whole section.** Don't
  fill it. Two full seconds of silence.
- **"That is the whole product" is said quietly.** It's the last line. Drop your
  voice, don't lift it.
- Don't rush. You have 26 seconds for 57 words, which is a comfortable pace with
  room to breathe.

## How to record

- **Landscape**, 1080p. Phone propped up on something, not held in your hand.
- **Light in front of your face** — sit facing a window or a lamp. Nothing bright
  behind your head, or you'll be a silhouette.
- **Chest up, centred in frame.** You get cropped to a circle, so leave some room
  around your head. Don't fill the frame with your face.
- Plain wall behind you. Plain clothes, no logos, no stripes.
- Quiet room. Fan off, AC off, notifications off.
- **Start recording, wait 2 seconds, then speak.** Leave 2 seconds of silence at
  the end too. That's what the dead air in the pacing table is for.
- Read it twice in one take with a three-second gap between, and the better one
  gets used.

**Your clip needs to be at least 30 seconds long.** If it's shorter than the
section, the circle goes blank at the end.
