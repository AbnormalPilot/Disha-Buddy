# Disha Buddy — 120-second video narration script

Record your **own real voice** to this script (the festival forbids AI-generated
voiceover). The on-screen captions are already timed to these lines, so read at a
natural, unhurried pace and you'll stay in sync. Total speaking time ≈ 100s,
leaving room for the ~8s team clip. **Hard cap: 120 seconds.**

Times are the scene's start → end within the final video.

---

### 1 · Introduction  (0:00 – 0:10)
> Meet Disha Buddy — a free mentor on Telegram that helps Indian students find the
> right college, and never miss a deadline.

### 2 · Problem  (0:10 – 0:29)
> Every year, fifteen lakh students chase college seats. But ninety-three percent
> of Indian schools have no career counsellor — one counsellor for every three
> thousand students. First-generation and small-town students miss deadlines,
> don't know real cutoffs or fees, and can't afford private guidance.

### 3 · Solution  (0:29 – 0:49)
> Disha changes that. It's a warm, Hinglish-speaking buddy on the app students
> already use. It remembers each student's marks, budget, and stream. It
> recommends colleges that genuinely fit — with cited sources, never false
> promises. And it texts first, before a counselling round closes.

### 4 · Demonstration  (0:49 – 1:15)
> Here's how it feels. A student messages in Hinglish — "seventy-eight percent
> aaye, budget one lakh." Disha replies in seconds with real colleges, fees, and
> cutoffs — each backed by a dated source. Days later, it reaches out on its own:
> "Riya, AKTU round two kal band ho raha hai." And a privacy-first CRM tracks
> every student and deadline — one-line summaries only, never full chats.

### 5 · Technologies  (1:15 – 1:32)
> Under the hood: an AI agent runtime with persistent memory, generative AI for
> natural Hinglish, and retrieval-augmented generation over a crawled college
> dataset — so every answer is grounded. It's built to run GPU-free on Intel's
> OpenVINO.

### 6 · Future scope  (1:32 – 1:44)
> What's next: all of India, more languages, WhatsApp, and scholarship matching —
> so every student has a mentor in their pocket. Disha Buddy — sahi disha, sahi
> decision.

### 7 · Team on camera  (1:44 – 1:52)
> [ Your recorded team clip — introduce yourselves and the project. ]

---

**Note on subtitles:** the narration is English, so subtitles aren't strictly
required by the rules — but the on-screen captions already serve as burned-in
English subtitles. If your team clip is spoken in Hindi/Hinglish, add English
subtitles to that clip.
